Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This site uses no cookies, neither mine nor anyone else's. The pages load nothing from other addresses, so no other company can set cookies through them.

What stays in your browser

The site keeps your preferences in the browser's local storage (localStorage), which only the site's own pages read. None of these values is sent to me.

NameWhat it keepsFor how long
themeThe theme you chose, light (light) or dark (dark).Until you clear the site's data
hueThe accent color you chose on the globe, an angle from 0 to 360.Until you clear the site's data
motionWhether the animations are paused (paused) or on (full).Until you clear the site's data
langThe language you chose (pt-BR or en-US). It is also how the Portuguese page knows you already said whether you want the English version.Until you clear the site's data
privacyYour choice in the privacy notice: whether anonymous measurement and session recording are on, the version of the purposes, and until when the choice counts.Six months

If you accept session recording, the browser also keeps the random codes of your recordings, for 30 days, so that you can delete them. Those codes go with each recording and with the request to delete it.

The anonymous measurement keeps nothing in the browser: the code of each page load exists only in the page's memory and is gone when the page closes. The Privacy Policy says what the measurement sends and why.

Why this needs no consent

These preferences only exist because you chose them, they stay on your device, and they let the site remember them. They do not identify you and are sent to no one. Session recording and the heat map, which depend on your consent, only start if you accept.

How to clear them

In your browser's settings, clear the data of the site eklund.com.br (usually under Privacy, Cookies and site data). The site goes back to its default theme, color and animations, and the privacy notice shows again.

Changes to this policy

When the site starts keeping anything else in your browser, this page changes first, and the date at the top shows when.
