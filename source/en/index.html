    02 Profile

    Seven years building software, five of them in payments. More than 10 years in IT overall.

    I'm a senior backend developer. I've worked at a digital bank, at a credit platform for banking correspondents and, since 2024, at OKTO Payments, a multinational payments company.

    At OKTO, the Brazil payments team rebuilt the company's Pix. OKTO is a direct participant in the SPI, the Central Bank system that settles Pix payments. We delivered sending and receiving Pix, key lookups, QR Code, Pix Automático and biometric Pix through Open Finance, all approved by the Central Bank.

    Before that, I led two teams, one of 4 people and one of 5. Each delivered a complete platform, from architecture and back end to apps, infrastructure and deployment.

    Day to day I write Go and TypeScript. I use DDD, Clean Architecture, CQRS and Event Sourcing to model systems, and Grafana, Loki, Prometheus and k6 to monitor and test them. I also develop with coding agents, such as Claude Code, Codex and Cursor, and write the specifications they follow.

    04 Load test

    I wrote a load test that runs in your browser.

    The test fires payments at a simplified Pix engine that I also wrote. Both run in the background of your browser, using Web Workers, and the output imitates k6, a load-testing tool. The engine validates each payment, rejects duplicates, tracks the balance of 10 thousand accounts and records everything in a hash-chained journal. The numbers are measured on your computer, on this visit.

    05 History

    Where I've worked.

    Four companies in seven years. The cards advance on their own, or click one to open it.

    OKTO Payments is a payments company operating in 7 countries and processing more than €17 billion a year. We rebuilt its Pix infrastructure so the company could be a direct participant in the SPI. Today that means more than 2 million transactions a day, with peaks of about 500 per second and 95% of responses within 200 ms.

    We went through the Central Bank certifications for receiving Pix, sending Pix, QR Code, key lookups on the DICT (with a token bucket), Recurring Scheduled Pix and Pix Automático. We also delivered biometric Pix through Open Finance (JSR) with ITP, and boletos through BaaS. We use Grafana, Loki and Prometheus dashboards to track success rate per client and latency percentiles, and we test load with k6.
